# BiliMerger Logo

使用内置 imagegen 生成，主题为「B 站粉色小电视 + 蓝色合并箭头」。PNG 保留透明通道；启动图标按实际图案边界居中并留出自适应裁切空间。

文件：`assets/icon.png`（应用内与 README）、`assets/icon_foreground.png`（自适应前景）、`assets/icon_background.png`（白色背景）、`assets/icon_legacy.png`（旧版启动图标）。各密度 Android 资源由 `dart run flutter_launcher_icons` 生成。

生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished minimal app logo for BiliMerger, an Android utility that merges Bilibili cached video and audio. Make its connection to Bilibili instantly recognizable through the iconic cute little television silhouette with two diagonal antennae and a bright Bilibili pink #FB7299 body. Within the television, use a white inset screen with two simple pink vertical eyes like the familiar Bilibili TV face. Integrate one small cyan #00A1D6 merge-arrow badge at the lower right: two clean paths converge into one right-facing arrow, drawn white on a solid cyan rounded badge, clearly suggesting merging media. Keep the television dominant and the merge badge subtle. Professional crisp flat vector-like geometry, bold generous rounded strokes, symmetrical TV, playful but clean, strong small-size legibility. No lettering, no words, no gradients, no 3D, no shadow, no extra decorations, no frame or app tile. Square 1024x1024 canvas with a genuinely transparent background (alpha channel). The complete mark including antennae and badge should occupy approximately the central 66% of canvas width and height, generous transparent padding on every side for Android adaptive icon safe area. Single centered finished icon, not a contact sheet.
